Noli: Trials of San Diego

A race through the town of San Diego. Land on a Character Checkpoint, choose a character card, and rise or fall by what you know of Rizal's novel.

Rules

  • Take turns rolling one die and moving that many spaces.
  • Normal spaces have no effect.
  • On a Character Checkpoint, pick 1 of 3 character cards and answer its question.
  • Easy ±2, Medium ±4, Hard ±6. Right answer: rise forward. Wrong answer: fall back.
  • Moving after a question never triggers another checkpoint.
  • The first player to reach space 50 wins.
