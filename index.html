Noli: Trials of San Diego

San Diego hides its secrets well. Roll the die, face its people, and prove you know their stories before the town decides your fate!

Normal space Character Checkpoint Finish (space 50)

Rules and scoring

  • Take turns rolling one die and moving that many spaces.
  • Normal spaces have no effect.
  • On a Character Checkpoint, pick 1 of 3 character cards and answer its question.
  • Right answer: rise forward. Wrong answer: fall back (never below space 1).
  • If you rise or fall onto another Character Checkpoint, it doesn't count. Your turn ends there.
  • The first player to reach space 50 wins. An exact roll is not needed.
